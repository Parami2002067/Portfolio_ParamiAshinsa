CareerLens AI

Business Analysis Journey

Business Problem

Many university students and fresh graduates struggle to understand the skills required by the technology industry. Academic education provides foundational knowledge, but students often lack visibility into current employer expectations, making it difficult to choose the right learning path.

Project Vision

To bridge the gap between academic education and industry requirements by providing data-driven career guidance and personalized skill recommendations.

Target Users / Stakeholders

Stakeholder Need
University Students Understand career options and required skills
Fresh Graduates Identify skill gaps before applying for jobs
Entry-level IT Professionals Plan career growth

Basic Business Questions

To ensure CareerLens AI addresses real user needs, the project began by identifying the key business questions that guide data analysis, feature development, and AI recommendations. Each question represents a problem that the platform aims to solve using evidence from real-world technology job market data.

Business Question Why it matters?
What are the most in-demand technology careers? Helps identify career opportunities based on real-world job market demand.
Which skills are frequently requested by employers? Identifies the core technical and professional skills expected by recruiters
How do skill requirements differ between career paths? Enables personalized recommendations for different IT careers.
How closely does a user's skills match industry requirements? Forms the basis for calculating a career readiness score.
Which skills should the user learn next? Supports personalized learning roadmaps.
What alternative career paths are suitable? Suggests related careers based on transferable skills.

Project Objectives

Based on the identified business problem and stakeholder needs, the main objectives of CareerLens AI :


  1. Identify Technology Career Opportunities

    2. Analyze real-world technology job postings to identify career domains, roles, and areas of demand within the technology industry.

  2. Identify Industry Skill Requirements

    3. Determine the technical and professional skills frequently requested by employers for different technology careers.

  3. Support Career Decision-Making

    4. Help students and early-career professionals understand which career paths align with their current skills and interests.

  4. Identify Individual Skill Gaps

    5. Compare a user's existing skills with industry requirements for a selected career path.

  5. Provide Personalized Learning Recommendations

    6. Recommend skills that users should develop to improve their readiness for their selected career.

  6. Recommend Alternative Career Paths

    7. Identify related career opportunities based on transferable skills and the user's existing capabilities.

  7. Provide Data-Driven Career Guidance

    8. Use evidence from real-world job-market data rather than relying solely on generalized career advice.
