CareerLens AI

Data Analytics & AI Journey

CareerLens AI is a data-driven career intelligence platform designed to analyze real-world technology job market data and identify the skills, career paths, and industry requirements demanded by employers.

This project focuses on transforming a large-scale job posting dataset into meaningful career insights through data exploration, technology role classification, skill analysis, and market trend identification. The extracted insights will support the development of personalized career recommendations for university students, fresh graduates, and entry-level IT professionals.

Standardizing Employer Skill Requirements

The original skills field contained semi-structured natural-language descriptions, making direct frequency analysis unreliable.

I developed a rule-based skill extraction pipeline using Python and regular expressions to map job descriptions to a standardized CareerLens skill knowledge base.

KEY INSIGHT

Technology Careers Require More Than Coding

The analysis shows that employer requirements span multiple competency areas. SQL and Python demonstrate strong demand for data and programming capabilities, while network security, quality assurance, web technologies, and professional problem-solving skills also appear prominently.

These findings provide the market-level foundation for CareerLens AI. The next stage will analyze skill demand within individual career domains and roles, enabling career-specific match scores and skill-gap recommendations.
